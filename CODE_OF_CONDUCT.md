# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming to everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give clear, actionable feedback.
- Focus on the code and its impact, not on people.
- Protect private data and avoid sharing credentials or captured traffic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private data, and other abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
